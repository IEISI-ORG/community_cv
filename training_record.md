# APNIC Academy Training Program Record

**Terry Sweetser — APNIC Training Delivery Manager, South Asia and Oceania (October 2022 – June 2025)**

During this tenure, APNIC delivered technical training across South Asia and Oceania under Terry's regional program leadership. Every event below falls within that organisational remit; events marked **(personal delivery)** were also personally trained by Terry. Co-trainer names are omitted here to keep this a record of program scope rather than individual colleague credit — see [README.md](README.md) for personally co-instructed engagements that are already individually credited there.

Source: [APNIC Academy public events record](https://academy.apnic.net/en/events), queried 13 October 2026 through the Academy events API, covering every face-to-face, hybrid and online event held in the two subregions from October 2022 to the PacIGF workshops of early July 2025. Dates are local to each event (the Academy stores times in UTC; each was converted using the event's own timezone).

---

## Summary

- **109 events** organised across South Asia and Oceania, October 2022 – June 2025
- **South Asia:** 71 events across 6 economies (Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka)
- **Oceania:** 37 events across 8 economies (Australia, Fiji, Guam, New Zealand, Papua New Guinea, Samoa, Tonga, Vanuatu), including 2 Pacific-wide online sessions
- **South Asia and Oceania combined:** 1 joint RPKI/ROV tutorial (hybrid, 169 registered)
- **Delivery:** 87 face to face, 6 hybrid, 16 online
- **30 events** personally delivered by Terry as trainer
- **79 events** delivered by the APNIC training team and Community Trainers under his program leadership
